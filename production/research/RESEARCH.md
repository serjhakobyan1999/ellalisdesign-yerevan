### Projects
- **Two-Storey Restaurant Project** (Behance) — "developed entirely from scratch, with a fresh design vision, and the full space was completed within four months"; second floor 205 m²; detailed measurements and site analysis before concept. Tools: 3ds Max, Corona, AutoCAD, ArchiCAD, Photoshop.
- **Restaurant Design Project** (Behance) — first floor 172 m²; "full transformation of a building that once served as a kindergarten into a contemporary restaurant concept"; chandeliers designed and 3D-modelled from the designer's original concept; curtains "move with light". Quote: "In this design, nothing is accidental…".
- **Design Visualization – Restaurant Courtyard** (Behance) — courtyard 308 m²; water flows beneath high-durability glass along the stairs into an open basin; steel and concrete base.
- **RESTAURANT DESIGN INTERIOR** (Behance) — a vertical video only.
- **Apartment, Nor Nork** (Instagram) — Nor Nork 5th district, "Nor Tun" residential complex, building 10; designed around the client's wishes; construction started.
- **Private house, Ajapnyak** (Instagram) — construction started; filmed for the supervision reel.

Quote used on the site: "Design is not only about aesthetics, but about shaping experiences and emotions within a space." (Behance project text).
