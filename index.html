The studio

“Design is not only about aesthetics, but about shaping experiences and emotions within a space.”

— Ellalis Design

Ellalis Design is a designer-led interior and exterior design studio in Yerevan. It develops private and public projects — apartments, private houses and restaurants — and stays with each one from the first concept to its realization on site.

Every project begins with detailed measurements and site analysis. The concept is shaped around the client’s wishes and the character of the building, then developed into photorealistic 3D visuals, so space, colour and function can be judged before construction starts.

Signature elements are drawn in-house. In the studio’s restaurant work, even the chandeliers were designed and 3D-modelled from an original concept — “nothing is accidental; every curve, every texture, every light and shadow has been intentionally placed.”

  • DisciplinesInterior & exterior design, 3D visualization

Services

A complete interior project, in one package.

From the first sketch to the finished room, one studio carries the idea through every stage — so nothing gets lost between design and construction.

  1. 01

    Full-service interior design

    Measurements, space planning, concept, materials, lighting, furniture and décor — a complete project developed around how you live, work or host.

  2. 02

    Exterior & courtyard design

    Outdoor spaces and courtyards designed as part of the same story — like a 308 m² restaurant courtyard with a glass-covered water staircase.

  3. 03

    Photorealistic 3D visualization

    Studio-grade renders that convey the atmosphere of the future space and let you evaluate layout, colour combinations and functional solutions before implementation.

  4. 04

    Construction supervision

    From interior design to construction supervision — the studio follows the work on site so the finished space matches the design.

  5. 05

    Bespoke details

    Sculpted ceilings, feature walls and custom lighting conceived and modelled from scratch for each project — the details that make a space unmistakable.

Two-Storey Restaurant

Type
Hospitality interior
Second floor
205 m²

A warm, cave-like sequence of rooms: terracotta plaster, sculpted stone ceilings and bronze slatted walls traced with light. Developed entirely from scratch with a fresh design vision — the full space was completed within four months.

02

From Kindergarten to Restaurant

Type
Adaptive reuse
First floor
172 m²
Scope
Full concept

The full transformation of a building that once served as a kindergarten into a contemporary restaurant: a central bar, sculpted relief walls, curtains that move with the light, and chandeliers designed and 3D-modelled from the studio’s original concept.

Type
Exterior
Courtyard

A spacious courtyard turned into the restaurant’s key feature — an outdoor leisure space of stone-clad walls and soft tulle. Water starts at the top of the staircase and flows visibly beneath high-durability glass along the steps into an open basin.
